# Maintainers

| Handle | Owns | Since |
|---|---|---|
| [@kalwei](https://github.com/kalwei) | everything | 2026-08-28 |

A maintainer is someone with merge rights and a named area. The area is what
they review first and release for; anything not listed falls to the founding
maintainer. Contributors — people with a merged pull request — are credited in
the release notes of the version that carried their change.
