    One row per part; one column per appraiser and trial. A reading already taken is shown and never overwritten. Study readings are never production checks and are on no control chart.

    Plan a study — every appraiser measures every part, each trial in an order they do not see

    Ten parts, three appraisers, three trials is the usual plan. Pick parts from across the range the process makes: parts that all look alike make any gauge look poor, and the study will say so rather than judge it.
